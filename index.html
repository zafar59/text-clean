Free Client-Side Text Sanitizer, AI Formatter & Invisible Character Stripper

Text Cleanup Studio is a deterministic, privacy-first text utility built for developers, AI prompt engineers, researchers, and writers. It sanitizes malformed copy, strips invisible zero-width bugs, removes conversational AI preambles, and standardizes whitespace with zero server logging.

Production-Ready Text Sanitization Features

Strip Invisible Unicode & Zero-Width Characters

Purges invisible unicode corruption such as zero-width spaces (\u200B), zero-width non-joiners (\u200C), byte-order marks (\uFEFF), soft hyphens (\u00AD), and hidden ASCII control characters that break JSON parsers, compilers, and database queries.

Remove AI Artifacts & Markdown Fences

Eliminates boilerplate ChatGPT and Claude pleasantries ("Certainly!", "Here is what you requested:", "Hope this helps!"), strips triple-backtick markdown fences while retaining indentation, and removes headers, blockquotes, and link markup.

Whitespace & Line Break Normalization

Trims edge whitespace per line, collapses multiple spaces into a single space, standardizes double spacing (\n{3,} to \n\n), and removes blank empty lines caused by PDF copy-pasting or text extraction tools.

Deduplicate Lines & Natural Sorting

Deduplicates identical lines while maintaining original order, or reorganizes lists into alphabetical, reverse, or natural numerical sorting without mangling line data.

Typography & Smart Quote Straightening

Normalizes curly quotes (“ ” ‘ ’) to standard ASCII quotes (" '), fixes em-dashes and ellipses, and formats casing to Sentence case, Title Case, UPPERCASE, lowercase, camelCase, or snake_case.

List ⇄ CSV & Delimiter Converter

Converts newline-separated records into comma-separated values (with optional quote wrapping) or converts CSV strings into itemized lines and markdown bullet lists in one click.

Why Browser-Based Client-Side Text Sanitization Matters

Most online text tools secretly upload your inputs to backend servers, creating compliance risks with confidential code, customer data, and proprietary drafts. Text Cleanup Studio processes 100% of your input within your browser's local sandbox memory. Zero server requests, telemetry, or cloud databases are involved, ensuring complete isolation and zero data leakage under GDPR, HIPAA, and internal security policies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Concise answers optimized for quick search solutions and featured snippets.

How do I remove extra line breaks from copied text?

To remove extra line breaks, paste your text into Text Cleanup Studio and activate 'Fix Double Spacing' or 'Remove Empty Lines'. The tool instantly collapses excessive newline sequences into single paragraph breaks without deleting required sentence spacing.

How do I strip ChatGPT and Claude markdown artifacts?

Select the 'AI Response Sanitizer' preset. It automatically removes conversational preambles like 'Certainly!' and closing pleasantries, un-nests code from triple-backtick fences while preserving indented syntax, and strips unnecessary asterisks and blockquotes.

Does this tool store or upload my text?

No. Text Cleanup Studio operates 100% client-side inside your web browser memory. Zero bytes are uploaded, transmitted, or logged on remote servers, ensuring strict privacy for sensitive code, proprietary datasets, and personal drafts.

How do I convert a list of items into comma-separated values?

Choose the 'List ⇄ CSV Tool' preset, or select 'Lines ➔ CSV' under Stage 4 Typography & Casing. The engine strips blank rows, trims whitespace around each item, deduplicates lines, and outputs a clean comma-separated string.

How do I detect and remove zero-width spaces online?

Paste your code or text into the input panel with 'Strip Zero-Width Characters' enabled. The regex engine targets invisible characters including zero-width spaces (\u200B), non-joiners (\u200C), byte-order marks (\uFEFF), and displays the count of purged invisible anomalies.
